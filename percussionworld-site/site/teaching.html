Approach

Teaching slagwerk

Every lesson builds on a broad foundation: techniek, reading, rhythmic dictation, theory en repertoire, across the whole family of slagwerk instruments.

For each instrument Michel Mordant works met a carefully selected set of methodes en solo literature, complemented by his own Start methodes for young beginners.

His students take deel in contests in Nederland, Duitsland, België en France, en many have become prize-winners en provincial of national champions. His slagwerk groups have given many concerts en won contests in Nederland en abroad.

Videos about his teaching can be found on Facebook.

How a leerling progresses

Every step en every explanation is written down. These two examples follow one kleine trom leerling through the first two years of lessons.

First year

Second year

The programma was expanded met slagwerk, marimba, rhythmic dictation en theory for the snare drummer, afdeling A (© Kreato). Practice time, age, talent en motivation determine the preparation for exams: two years for good students, three for less diligent ones.

Exam programma: Etude A1 en A2, Voordracht A1 en A2 (G. Bomhof); Het Debuut (S. Egberts); A-mix for claves en bongo’s (G. Bomhof).

Also required: imitation (in 4/4, waltz en 6/8 march), sight reading, playing from memory, rudiments 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11 en 20, roll exercises (S. Egberts), en correcting mistakes by ear en from notation.
