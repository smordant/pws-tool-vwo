Approach

Teaching Schlagzeug

Every lesson builds on a broad foundation: Technik, reading, rhythmic dictation, theory und repertoire, across the whole family of Schlagzeug instruments.

For each instrument Michel Mordant works mit a carefully selected set of Schulen und solo literature, complemented by his own Start Schulen for young beginners.

His students take Teil in contests in Niederlande, Deutschland, Belgien und France, und many have become prize-winners und provincial oder national champions. His Schlagzeug groups have given many concerts und won contests in Niederlande und abroad.

Videos about his teaching can be found on Facebook.

How a Schüler progresses

Every step und every explanation is written down. These two examples follow one kleine Trommel Schüler through the first two years of lessons.

First year

Second year

The Programm was expanded mit Schlagzeug, Marimba, rhythmic dictation und theory for the snare drummer, Stufe A (© Kreato). Practice time, age, talent und motivation determine the preparation for exams: two years for good students, three for less diligent ones.

Exam Programm: Etude A1 und A2, Voordracht A1 und A2 (G. Bomhof); Het Debuut (S. Egberts); A-mix for Claves und Bongos (G. Bomhof).

Also required: imitation (in 4/4, waltz und 6/8 march), sight reading, playing from memory, rudiments 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11 und 20, roll exercises (S. Egberts), und correcting mistakes by ear und from notation.
