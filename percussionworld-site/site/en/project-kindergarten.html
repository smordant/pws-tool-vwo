Projects  /  Germany

Project in Germany

Kindergarten

Musical repertoire, played on unconventional instruments.

In an afternoon working group, pupils explore which everyday objects from their own world — pots, pans, brooms … — and which sounds of their own bodies can be used to make music. Rhythm is at the centre.

The group develops ideas for the musical realisation, experiments, rehearses rhythm pieces devised together and presents the results to an audience.

In this way pupils are encouraged to engage creatively with their everyday surroundings — a counterweight to the many cognitive school subjects.

Making music together across year groups and classes greatly strengthens the bond between very different pupils (age, cultural background, abilities …) and creates a sense of “we” that also helps them identify with their school.

Michel Mordant

Percussionist, composer, conductor and percussion teacher, working in the Netherlands and Germany.
