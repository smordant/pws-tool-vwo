Ensembles  /  Kreato Thorn

Netherlands · 2006 – 2011

Percussion Ensemble Music School Kreato, Thorn

A percussion ensemble of soloists and young talent, performing at festivals, the Day of Percussion and in projects with wind ensembles. Biography (PDF)

  1. 2011

    11 February · Geleen

    Presentation of the Piet Gieberts medal. Response from Cor Roumen (Sjoutvotte organisation)

    Ensemble: Frank Evers, Kerim Karatas, Wiek Maessen, Sander Arts.

    • Jovial Jasper (soloist Frank Evers) — George Hamilton Green, arr. Bob Becker
    • 4 Rambling — Michel Mordant
    • Souvenir du Circus Renz (soloist Kerim Karatas) — Gustav Peters, arr. Michel Mordant
  2. 2010

    12 December · Rotterdam

    Day of Percussion in cooperation with PAS NL and Codarts Conservatory Rotterdam. Ensemble: Frank Evers, Kerim Karatas, Rik Houben, Wiek Maessen.

    • Rainbows — Alice Gomez
    • The Whistler (soloist Frank Evers) — George Hamilton Green, arr. Bob Becker
    • 4 Rambling — Michel Mordant

    31 October · Posterholt

    In cooperation with Harmonie Wilhelmina.

    Ensemble: Bianca and Joyce Berkers, Cyril Graus, Luuk Heijmans, Myrthe Peulen, Roger Scheepers, Stan van Landeghem, Stan Voncken, Wiek Maessen — Balalaikan Holiday (Morris A. Brand); To the Top (Wibrand van Norel).

    Ensemble: Tymen Abels, Bob van Ninhuijs, Dion Lamkin, Edo van Bunningen, Frank Evers, Kerim Karatas, Rik Houben, Ruud Joris, Wiek Maessen — Jovial Jasper (soloist Frank Evers, G. H. Green, arr. Bob Becker); 4 Rambling (Michel Mordant).

    With Harmonie Wilhelmina: Brass Connection (Vincent Cox).

  3. 2009

    Ensemble: Tymen Abels, Bob Bocken, Dion Lamkin, Edo van Bunningen, Kerim Karatas, Rik Houben, Ruud Joris, Frank Evers.

    “De Auw Kerk”, Burchtstraat 2, Bunde

    Concert in cooperation with the wind ensemble Cupros:

    • Cha Cha Rufus — Terry O’Malley
    • Jovial Jasper (soloist Dion) — G. H. Green, arr. Bob Becker
    • Jazzy Jungle Sound — Maurice Schipper
    • A La Samba — Mitchell Peters
    • Brass Connection — Vincent Cox

    Heel · Percussion Festival

    • A La Samba — Mitchell Peters
    • Ghanaian Theme (soloist Tymen) — Matthias Schmitt, arr. Peter Sadlo
    • Log Cabin Blues (soloist Kerim) — G. H. Green, arr. Bob Becker
    • Brazilian Street Dances — Thomas O’Connor
  4. 2008

    Ensemble: Tymen Abels, Bob Bocken, Dion Lamkin, Edo van Bunningen, Kerim Karatas, Rik Houben, Ruud Joris, Tim Aerdts.

    16 November · Tilburg

    PAS Day of Percussion: Schmitt, Ghanaia — marimba solo (Tymen) with percussion trio; Green/Becker, Log Cabin Blues (soloist Kerim); O’Connor, Brazilian Street Dances; Rafael — solo concert.

    15 November · Maastricht

    Percussion day, Music School Maastricht: Schmitt, Ghanaia (Tymen) with percussion trio; Green/Becker, Log Cabin Blues (soloist Kerim); Rafael — Cello Suite.

    Video: Kreato Slagwerk Ensemble Thorn — Brazilian Street Dances, Thomas O’Connor

  5. 2007

    Ensemble: Tymen Abels, Edo van Bunningen, Stan Hoeymakers, Ruud Joris, Rafael Sars, Johan Veugelers, Stefan van de Wouw.

    Thorn · Muziekcentrale Adams — Maasgouw presentation

    Schmitt, Ghanaia — marimba solo (Rafael) with percussion trio; Long, Birth of a King; O’Connor, Brazilian Street Dances.

  6. 2006

    Ensemble: Tymen Abels, Marco Cremers, Stan Hoeymakers, Luc Houben, Danny Joosten, Rafael Sars, Dyon Verstegen, Johan Veugelers, Stefan van de Wouw.

    10 September · Thorn

    Heritage Day (Monumentendag): Živković, Trio per Uno; Glennie, Prayer; Van der Kolk, Procussion.

    17 March · Heythuysen

    Exhibition of the Kreato visual arts department (premiere concert): Živković, Trio per Uno; Glennie, Prayer; Van der Kolk, Procussion.
