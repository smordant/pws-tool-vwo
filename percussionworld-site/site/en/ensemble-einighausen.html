Ensembles  /  Einighausen

Netherlands · from 2007

Fanfare Concordia Einighausen

Percussion ensemble and youth drum band of Fanfare Concordia Einighausen, taught by Michel Mordant from 26 January 2007.

  1. 2010

    2 December · Einighausen

    Percussion ensemble: Mexican Dance (J. Wassen); Western Movie (Henk Martens); Go Tell It on the Mountain (trad.); You’re So Cool (H. Zimmer, arr. F. Kessels).

    Youth drum band: Impressions (Jo Corstjens); Largo (Antonín Dvořák, arr. Michel Mordant); a work by Vincent Cox.

    21 November · contest Puth-Schinnen

    Mexican Dance (J. Wassen); Western Movie (Henk Martens); Go Tell It on the Mountain (trad.); You’re So Cool (H. Zimmer, arr. F. Kessels).

    30 May · concert Guttecoven “Maasgouw”

    Predicate “very good”: Magnificent People (W. Norel); Western Movie (Henk Martens); You’re So Cool (H. Zimmer, arr. F. Kessels).

    27 February · Dutch championships, Dronten

    Magnificent People (W. Norel); You’re So Cool (H. Zimmer, arr. F. Kessels); Io, the Moon and the Giant (S. Kamstra).

  2. 2009

    Repertoire: Magnificent People (W. Norel); You’re So Cool (H. Zimmer, arr. F. Kessels); Io, the Moon and the Giant (S. Kamstra).

    • 22 November — LBT contest, Kerkrade: Winner 2nd division · contest winner (PDF) · photos · Limburgs Dagblad · Trompetter
    • 8 November — concert, Maasbracht
    • 14 June — concert, Eschweiler (Germany) · Eschweiler Nachrichten
    • 7 June — concert, Grevenbicht “Maasgouw”
    • 16 May — concert, Vossem (Belgium)
    • 19 April — Obbicht, judge Henrico Stevens: Winner 3rd division
  3. 2008

    Repertoire: Another Dimension (Henk Smit); Io (W. Kamstra); Irish Impressions, part 1 (Henk Martens).

    • 12 April — Einighausen, judge Henrico Stevens — also Concordia on Parade (M. Mordant)
    • 13 April — concert, Einighausen
    • 18 May — Maasgouw, Berg aan de Maas
    • 7 September — Drumband festival, Holtum
  4. 2007

    17 December · Einighausen

    Christmas concert: Jingle Bells, Little Drummer Boy, with the recorder training group.

    20 October · Schouwburg Sittard

    “Then it was our drum band’s turn. I think they were the surprise of the evening for many in the audience — and certainly for the members of the fanfare. We knew they were working in a different way, but that they could already show us this surprised many. What an atmosphere in Io, and what a virtuoso finale with the Sabre Dance. Ladies and gentlemen of the drum band, our compliments — and certainly also to instructor Michel Mordant.”

    2 June · Maasgouw tournament, Nieuwstadt

    Concordia on Parade (M. Mordant); Soundtracks (L. Camp, arr. M. Mordant).

Michel Mordant

Percussionist, composer, conductor and percussion teacher, working in the Netherlands and Germany.
