Projects  /  Netherlands

Project in the Netherlands

‘Stomp At School’

The workshop for designing, building and playing percussion instruments! In six weeks the children learn to make a design, work it out and play it.

This is how children develop insight in several areas at once.

They combine design, spatial awareness, abstract thinking, project work and learning to plan with creativity, a sense of rhythm and presentation.

Also possible: a small concert in a care home, kindergarten or primary school.

  1. Lesson 1

    The teacher explains the process and introduces the possibilities and materials. Demonstration of shakers, sticks, bucket drums and rattles. Testing of hearing and sense of rhythm through simple call-and-response clapping (quarter and eighth notes) in groups of four beats.

  2. Lesson 2

    Starting to make shakers. Needed: an empty can, small pebbles or similar material (e.g. dried peas, rice), scissors, glue and cardboard to seal the opening.

    Optional finishing touches: a drawing glued to the can (make sure can and drawing are the same size), stickers, or colouring with felt-tip pens.

  3. Lesson 3

    The children’s own instrument designs and the materials they have brought. The instruments are made with the teacher’s help.

  4. Lesson 4

    Building the instrument: assembling the prepared parts (filling the can, sealing it with glue, letting everything dry well and checking it). Optionally a drawing, stickers or felt-tip pens.

  5. Lesson 5

    Finishing off and explaining how to play the shaker. Making music with body percussion and/or the home-made shakers. Call and response (ear training) with shakers, and playing along with music.

  6. Lesson 6

    Rehearsal and final presentation for fellow pupils, teachers and parents. Evaluation with the whole group.
