Projects  /  Germany

Project in Germany

Klanggarten

Sound explorers and instrument builders experiment with sounds and rhythms — installing a “sound garden”.

The project gives children the opportunity to explore the world of sounds and tones and sharpens their awareness of listening.

The children experiment with the sound of different materials, build instruments and use them rhythmically and musically according to their sound qualities. In doing so they explore properties of sound such as volume, duration and pitch.

When making sound objects, materials and everyday items can be combined. Creativity comes first, so that unconventional and original new creations can emerge, to which every participating child contributes ideas, knowledge and skills — including in the artistic design.

The sound garden

In a second phase, sound sculptures are created collectively from self-made and found sound objects and installed permanently in a “sound garden” in the school grounds (playground or garden). Self-built string, wind and percussion instruments and other sound-producing objects are assembled into sound installations according to the children’s own ideas and plans.

The sound garden can then be used to experiment with sound and to make music — improvised as well as in set forms.

The whole project combines music-educational, artistic and craft aspects; social learning and sustainability are also essential parts of the work.

Michel Mordant

Percussionist, composer, conductor and percussion teacher, working in the Netherlands and Germany.
