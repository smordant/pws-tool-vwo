Projects  /  Netherlands

Project in the Netherlands

Workshop Percussie

A one-hour interactive workshop in which fifteen percussion instruments are presented, explained and played together with the audience.

  • Duration1 hour (possibly a little longer)
  • VenueA room the size of a classroom
  • Set-upChairs in a U-shape (no tables); tables with the instruments on display behind the teacher

Instruments

Tambourine and beatring, sleigh bells, agogo bells, claves, maracas and cabasa, guiro and shaker-guiro, vibraslap, pair of cymbals, gong, temple blocks, bongos, suspended cymbals (played on the edge, the bow and the cup), cowbell, triangle, djembe — with sticks of wood, felt and yarn.

Explanation and playing techniques

Each instrument is presented, explained and demonstrated, in this order:

  1. Tambourine and beatring — explaining the difference (a beatring has no head)
  2. Sleigh bells
  3. Agogo bells
  4. Claves
  5. Maracas and cabasa
  6. Guiro and shaker-guiro
  7. Vibraslap
  8. Pair of cymbals — struck and rubbed
  9. Gong — with the fist and a beater; stroking the rim with an iron and a wooden stick
  10. Temple blocks — sticks of wood, felt and yarn
  11. Bongos — by hand and with different sticks (wood, felt and yarn)
  12. Suspended cymbals — played on the edge, the bow and the cup; sticks of wood, felt and yarn
  13. Cowbell
  14. Triangle
  15. Djembe

Warm-up exercise

Playing along in 4/4: stamp your feet on beats 1 and 3, clap on 2 and 4.

Concept

The instruments are handed out and played together with the audience — as well as everyone can.

Photos of the workshop in Maastricht, with the assistance of Myrthe and Sander, will follow.
