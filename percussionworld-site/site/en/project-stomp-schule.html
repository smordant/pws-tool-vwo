Project in Germany

Stomp in der Schule

A year plan for pupils aged 10 to 14: body percussion, self-built instruments, rap and performances — at the care home, at the Gesamtschule and at the summer fair.

Organisation

Craft materials: scissors, glue, needles and thread (for sewing), glue clamps, knives, nails, hammer, cans, buckets.

Instruments: brooms, buckets and pots, dustpan and brush, balls, bins.

Facilities: lockable cupboard, sound system. To borrow for special lessons: DVD player and television.

Exceptions: go outside in fine weather — but mind the noise for other lessons (bins, buckets, stair railings, feet, balls, brooms and hand brushes).

Preparation: bring newspapers to put under the craft work.
