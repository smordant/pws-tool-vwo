Schutterij St. Stephanus Dieteren

Percussion ensemble of Schutterij St. Stephanus Dieteren, taught by Michel Mordant from 24 August 2007. Several of its soloists became Limburg and Dutch champions — see student achievements.

    Section B-a: 2nd place, 3rd division — Close Up (Jo Zinzen).
