Areas of work

A life in percussion

Composition, conducting, teaching and adjudication — four disciplines that inform one another and share a single aim: making percussion music come alive for players and audiences.

Composer

Solo, duo and ensemble works for drumset, snare drum, keyboard percussion and timpani, registered with B.U.M.A. and performed at contests and concerts.

Workshop Percussie

A one-hour hands-on introduction to fifteen percussion instruments, ending in a performance together with the audience.

Percussive Arts Society

PAS Netherlands Chapter

Michel Mordant is President of the Netherlands Chapter of the Percussive Arts Society and co-host of the Dutch Day of Percussion — the national percussion day held since 2006 in Maastricht, Voerendaal, Tilburg, Vught, Rotterdam, Kerkrade, The Hague, Thorn, Zwolle, Eindhoven and Sittard.
