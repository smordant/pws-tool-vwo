  2. 2009

    A new instructor for the drum band

    From the society’s newsletter, 7 September 2009 (translated from Dutch):

    On Saturday 5 September the summer break ended for the drum band, and rehearsals resumed — with a fresh start under a new instructor. After more than sixteen years under the direction of John Maassen, the band will from now on be led by Michel Mordant.

    John had announced last year that he wished to stop. A working group consisting of vice-chairman Hub Renet, board member Tilly Vola-Stoffels and drum band member Kirsten Theunissen then set out to find a new instructor. The group went about this very carefully, but setbacks meant it took somewhat longer than planned. On Friday 28 August 2009 — after approval by the board in a specially convened meeting — a final, positive meeting took place with Michel Mordant, our new instructor.

    On Saturday evening Michel was introduced to the drum band and gave his first rehearsal. Michel is 47, married with two children, and is also instructor of the drum band of Fanfare Concordia in Einighausen. He also teaches at Kreato in Thorn and at the music school in Eschweiler. He had already been active with us as a jury member during a trio concert in 2006.

    We wish Michel and the whole drum band every success and a pleasant and fruitful collaboration. In a coming issue of the Cecielke the editors will try to find out more about him. Our sincere thanks go to John for his dedication over the past years; he will be given a fitting farewell at a later date.
