Approach

Teaching percussion

Every lesson builds on a broad foundation: technique, reading, rhythmic dictation, theory and repertoire, across the whole family of percussion instruments.

For each instrument Michel Mordant works with a carefully selected set of methods and solo literature, complemented by his own Start methods for young beginners.

His students take part in contests in the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France, and many have become prize-winners and provincial or national champions. His percussion groups have given many concerts and won contests in the Netherlands and abroad.

Videos about his teaching can be found on Facebook.

Second year

The programme was expanded with percussion, marimba, rhythmic dictation and theory for the snare drummer, level A (© Kreato). Practice time, age, talent and motivation determine the preparation for exams: two years for good students, three for less diligent ones.

Exam programme: Etude A1 and A2, Voordracht A1 and A2 (G. Bomhof); Het Debuut (S. Egberts); A-mix for claves and bongos (G. Bomhof).

Also required: imitation (in 4/4, waltz and 6/8 march), sight reading, playing from memory, rudiments 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11 and 20, roll exercises (S. Egberts), and correcting mistakes by ear and from notation.
