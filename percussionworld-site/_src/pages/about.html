Biography

About Michel Mordant

Percussionist, timpanist, composer, conductor and teacher — rooted in the musical life of Limburg and active on both sides of the Dutch–German border.

Michel Mordant studied percussion at the Conservatory of Maastricht (Academy of Music, the Netherlands), graduating in 1991.

His principal teachers were Ruud Wiener (mallets), Hay Beurskens (snare drum), Pieter Jansen (snare drum and mallets), Werner Otten (timpani) and Chris Dekker (drumset). He further broadened his musicianship through lessons and master classes with Ruud Wiener in mallet improvisation (Switzerland) and with Jim Chapin on snare drum.

As a freelance percussionist he has performed with renowned orchestras in the Netherlands and abroad, among them the Limburgs Symfonie Orkest, the Johann Strauss Orkest and the Städtisches Orchester Eschweiler, as well as drummer with the Young Jazz Cats (Germany). Today he is timpanist of the Symphonie Orchester Kerkrade.

Alongside his performing career he teaches percussion at the music schools Cultuurperron in Thorn and Stichting Opmaat in Schinnen (the Netherlands), and at the Jugendmusikschule and Gesamtschule Eschweiler and the Jugendmusikschule Heinsberg (Germany).

Michel Mordant has written a substantial body of works for percussion, from teaching pieces and contest solos to ensemble music. He serves on the jury at band contests and examinations, and is involved in many other activities — workshops, school projects and the Netherlands Chapter of the Percussive Arts Society, of which he is President.

Beginnings

Musical roots in Vijlen

Michel started out as a trombone player with St. Martinus Vijlen, taught by Th. Janssen and A. Mullenders. In 1979 he changed to timpani, studying with B. Flas, and took mallet and snare drum lessons with H. Martens and T. Winkens (drum band). From 1982 to 1986 he played snare drum in the Drum and Bugle Corps St. Joseph Vijlen.

His grandfather, Michel Mohnen — a harmonica player who lived in Vijlen from 1907 to 1989 — made Michel’s very first drumsticks. Together with his mother, he was the most important inspiration for Michel’s musical career.
