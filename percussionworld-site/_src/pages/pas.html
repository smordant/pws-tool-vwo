22 October 2023 · Sittard

Day of Percussion 2023

Presented by Percussion Friends and the Percussive Arts Society: the 10th Dutch Percussion Day (Nederlandse Slagwerkdag), 10.00–22.00, at Gemeenschapshuis Broeksittard, Dorpstraat 1, 6137 SR Sittard. Free admission, with workshops by Ramon Lormans, Maikel Claessens and Georgi Tsenov; performances by drum bands and percussion groups from Sittard and the surrounding area and the Musikschule Eschweiler; closing concert by Mallet Collective. Musical hosts: Fanfare St. Caecilia and Michel Mordant.

2022

Chapter activities 2022

In 2022 there were no chapter concerts such as a Day of Percussion. The chapter was present at the World Music Contest in Kerkrade with promotional material, membership forms and other PAS material:

  • 9 July — Percussion Contest, Theatre Kerkrade
  • 23–24 July — March and Show Contest, Parkstad Limburg Stadion (Roda JC)
  • 30–31 July — March and Show Contest, Parkstad Limburg Stadion (Roda JC)
Chapter log and reporting forms 2022

Activity, business and financial reports submitted to PAS for 2022 (no PAS members in attendance were registered).

29 March 2020 · Eindhoven

Day of Percussion 2020

The 10th Dutch Percussion Day was planned for 29 March 2020, 10.00–18.00, at Fontys Sporthogeschool, Theo Koomenlaan 3, 5644 HZ Eindhoven — and was cancelled because of the corona pandemic.

Planned performers: Alex Jacobowitz, Tatiana Koleva, and the Eric Aalbers percussion ensemble conducted by Paul Doom. Musical hosts: Jelle Roeper and Michel Mordant. CGN United

10 March 2018 · Zwolle

Day of Percussion 2018

9th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–18.00, at ArtEZ, Aan de Stadsmuur 88, 8011 VD Zwolle.

With Niek Kleinjan, Traditional Collective Drum Corps, Jan Wolfkamp, Wim de Vries and others, including KTK and Koen Herfst. Musical hosts: Peter Berk and Michel Mordant.

7 March 2015 · Ittervoort

Day of Percussion 2015

8th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–18.00, at Muziekcentrale Adams, Aziëstraat 17–19, Ittervoort (Thorn). With various drum bands, percussion groups and ensembles. Sheet music took centre stage, with several publishers present. Musical hosts: Albert Staten and Michel Mordant.

Sponsored by Van Vught Slagwerk, PaBo-Music and others.

6 May 2012 · The Hague

Day of Percussion 2012

7th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–17.00, at the Royal Conservatoire (Koninklijk Conservatorium), Juliana van Stolberglaan 1, 2595 CA The Hague.

With Miguel Rodriguez, Slagwerkgroep Den Haag, Percossa, Davai Percussion, Ruud Wiener and percussionists of the Residentie Orkest. Workshops, master classes, demonstrations, stands, performances by various ensembles and presentations by conservatory students — ragtimes, percussion trios, solo marimba repertoire, African workshops and more. Musical hosts: Hans Zonderop and Michel Mordant.

Sponsored by Van Vught Slagwerk, PaBo-Music, Van der Plas, Van Groningen and others.
