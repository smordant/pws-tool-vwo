Ensembles  /  Vijlen

Netherlands · 1999 – 2006

Drum and Bugle Corps St. Joseph Vijlen

Michel Mordant played snare drum in this band from 1982 until 1986. He started teaching the corps on 1 October 1999 and stopped on 20 August 2006.
