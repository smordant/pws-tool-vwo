8	Approach
9	Teaching percussion
10	Every lesson builds on a broad foundation: technique, reading, rhythmic dictation, theory and repertoire, across the whole family of percussion instruments.
11	For each instrument Michel Mordant works with a carefully selected set of methods and solo literature, complemented by his own <1>Start methods</1> for young beginners.
12	His students take part in contests in the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France, and many have become <1>prize-winners and provincial or national champions</1>. His <2>percussion groups</2> have given many concerts and won contests in the Netherlands and abroad.
13	Videos about his teaching can be found on <1>Facebook</1>.

169	Second year
170	The programme was expanded with percussion, marimba, rhythmic dictation and theory for the snare drummer, level A (© Kreato). Practice time, age, talent and motivation determine the preparation for exams: two years for good students, three for less diligent ones.
171	<1>Exam programme:</1> Etude A1 and A2, Voordracht A1 and A2 (G. Bomhof); <2>Het Debuut</2> (S. Egberts); <3>A-mix</3> for claves and bongos (G. Bomhof).
172	<1>Also required:</1> imitation (in 4/4, waltz and 6/8 march), sight reading, playing from memory, rudiments 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11 and 20, roll exercises (S. Egberts), and correcting mistakes by ear and from notation.
