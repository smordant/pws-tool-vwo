34	22 October 2023 · Sittard
35	Day of Percussion 2023
36	Presented by Percussion Friends and the Percussive Arts Society: the 10th Dutch Percussion Day (Nederlandse Slagwerkdag), 10.00–22.00, at Gemeenschapshuis Broeksittard, Dorpstraat 1, 6137 SR Sittard. Free admission, with workshops by Ramon Lormans, Maikel Claessens and Georgi Tsenov; performances by drum bands and percussion groups from Sittard and the surrounding area and the Musikschule Eschweiler; closing concert by Mallet Collective. Musical hosts: Fanfare St. Caecilia and Michel Mordant.

46	Day of Percussion 2020
47	The 10th Dutch Percussion Day was planned for 29 March 2020, 10.00–18.00, at Fontys Sporthogeschool, Theo Koomenlaan 3, 5644 HZ Eindhoven — and was cancelled because of the corona pandemic.
48	Planned performers: Alex Jacobowitz, Tatiana Koleva, and the Eric Aalbers percussion ensemble conducted by Paul Doom. Musical hosts: Jelle Roeper and Michel Mordant. <1>CGN United</1>

50	10 March 2018 · Zwolle
51	Day of Percussion 2018
52	9th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–18.00, at <1>ArtEZ</1>, Aan de Stadsmuur 88, 8011 VD Zwolle.
53	With Niek Kleinjan, Traditional Collective Drum Corps, Jan Wolfkamp, Wim de Vries and others, including KTK and Koen Herfst. Musical hosts: Peter Berk and Michel Mordant.
54	7 March 2015 · Ittervoort
55	Day of Percussion 2015
56	8th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–18.00, at Muziekcentrale Adams, Aziëstraat 17–19, Ittervoort (Thorn). With various drum bands, percussion groups and ensembles. Sheet music took centre stage, with several publishers present. Musical hosts: Albert Staten and Michel Mordant.
57	Sponsored by Van Vught Slagwerk, PaBo-Music and others.
58	6 May 2012 · The Hague
59	Day of Percussion 2012
60	7th Dutch Percussion Day, 10.00–17.00, at the Royal Conservatoire (<1>Koninklijk Conservatorium</1>), Juliana van Stolberglaan 1, 2595 CA The Hague.
61	With Miguel Rodriguez, Slagwerkgroep Den Haag, Percossa, Davai Percussion, Ruud Wiener and percussionists of the Residentie Orkest. Workshops, master classes, demonstrations, stands, performances by various ensembles and presentations by conservatory students — ragtimes, percussion trios, solo marimba repertoire, African workshops and more. Musical hosts: Hans Zonderop and Michel Mordant.
62	Sponsored by Van Vught Slagwerk, PaBo-Music, Van der Plas, Van Groningen and others.
